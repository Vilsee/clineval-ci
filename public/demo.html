From commit to production, gated.

A clinical AI merge gate, provisioned with Terraform, built and tested by Jenkins, and deployed to the cloud only when it passes.

Keys: 1 to 5 jump to a section, T starts the timer, S runs the gate simulation.

Terraform builds the infrastructure.

Two modules and a resource group, with typed variables, outputs and optional remote state. Every file below is the real source.
